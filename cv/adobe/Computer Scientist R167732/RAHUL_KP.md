## RESEARCH & PUBLICATIONS

### Neural Collaborative Filtering for Movie Recommendation

**Research / Preprint · 2026**

**Preprint DOI:** `10.20944/preprints202605.0449.v1`

- Designed and implemented a Neural Collaborative Filtering (NCF) movie recommendation system using both a from-scratch NumPy implementation and PyTorch.
- Implemented neural-network forward propagation and manual backpropagation to establish a mathematical baseline before framework benchmarking.
- Evaluated GMF + MLP architectures on MovieLens 1M with implicit-feedback training, 1:4 negative sampling, leave-one-out evaluation, 99 negative candidates, Hit@10, and NDCG@10.
- Achieved comparable recommendation quality between scratch and PyTorch implementations while obtaining approximately **12.8× faster epoch execution** with PyTorch.
- Extended the research into a production architecture using FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Docker, Nginx, monitoring, and Kubernetes-oriented deployment.
- Manuscript submitted to IEEE Transactions on Knowledge and Data Engineering (TKDE).
